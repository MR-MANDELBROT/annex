Privacy

aOS has no server and collects nothing.

aOS runs on your Mac and your iPhone. This is everything it sends, stores and sees.

  • No analytics

    aOS sends no analytics, crash reports or usage data anywhere. There is no account to create.

  • Downloads

    aOS downloads macOS from Apple, Google Chrome from Google (inside the workspace) and its own updates from this website’s release feed on GitHub. Those servers see a normal download request.

  • Your agent’s credentials

    aOS never reads, stores or forwards the credentials of your agents or your accounts. Signing in to an agent always happens in the agent’s own flow.

  • Sign-ins you approve

    A sign-in you approve goes once from your iPhone or Mac to the workspace, over SSH, and is typed into that one website. aOS doesn’t keep it.

  • Bitwarden

    If you use Bitwarden, your passwords stay in your Bitwarden account. aOS uses one only when you approve it, for that one sign-in.

  • On your Mac

    Everything aOS stores stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/aOS: the workspace, its keys, logs and the exchange folder. Deleting that folder removes it all.

  • aOS for iPhone

    The iPhone app talks to your Mac directly over SSH, with a key that never leaves the iPhone. There is no server and no account of ours in between.

  • Notifications

    Notifications about requests reach your iPhone through Apple. They name the agent and the reason, never a password.

  • What your agent sees

    Your agent sees screenshots of the workspace while it works. What happens to them is governed by your agent’s provider and its terms.

  • This website

    This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes your IP address to deliver it. The site itself sets no cookies and loads no trackers or external fonts.
