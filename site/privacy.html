Privacy

Annex has no server and collects nothing.

  • Annex sends no analytics, crash reports or usage data anywhere.
  • It downloads macOS from Apple, Google Chrome from Google (inside the workspace) and updates for itself from this website's release feed. Those servers see a normal download request.
  • It never reads, stores or forwards credentials of your agents or your accounts. Signing in to an agent always happens in the agent's own flow.
  • Everything Annex stores stays on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Annex: the workspace, its keys, logs and the exchange folder. Deleting that folder removes it all.
  • What your agent does with screenshots of the workspace is governed by your agent's provider and its terms.
